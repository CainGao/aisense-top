Code understanding is the new frontier. Today's top two repos — Understand-Anything and CodeGraph — both tackle the same problem from different angles: giving AI agents structured knowledge of existing codebases. After months of "generate more code" tools, the pendulum is swinging toward "understand what you have." Meanwhile, Karpathy's skills file (154K stars) and ECC (192K stars) prove that the "meta-AI" category — tools that make AI agents themselves better — has become a massive market segment. The agent ecosystem is maturing past raw generation into comprehension, optimization, and self-improvement.
