Cognition Raises $1B at $25B Pre-Money Valuation

AI coding startup Cognition, maker of the autonomous coding agent Devin, has closed a $1 billion funding round at a $25 billion pre-money valuation. The round underscores investor confidence in AI-powered software development tools and signals that the market for AI coding agents is accelerating rapidly. Cognition's Devin has been competing with GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and other AI dev tools.

ElevenLabs Unveils Music Generation Model with Mid-Track Genre Switching

ElevenLabs, best known for its AI voice synthesis, has launched a new music generation model capable of seamlessly switching genres mid-track. The model represents a significant leap in AI music creation, enabling artists and creators to produce dynamic, multi-genre compositions from text prompts. This puts ElevenLabs in direct competition with Suno, Udio, and Google's MusicFX.

YouTube Automatically Labels AI-Generated Videos

YouTube has begun automatically detecting and labeling AI-generated content on its platform. The new system uses detection algorithms to identify synthetic media and applies disclosure labels, addressing growing concerns about AI-generated misinformation. This move comes as regulators worldwide push for greater transparency in AI-generated content.

Robinhood Launches AI Agent Stock Trading API

Robinhood has rolled out a new API that allows AI agents to autonomously execute stock trades on behalf of users. The feature enables developers to build AI-powered trading agents that can analyze market conditions and execute trades programmatically. The announcement has sparked debate about the implications of autonomous AI agents in financial markets.

China Increasingly Keeping Its Best AI Talent at Home

A new analysis reveals that China is successfully retaining more of its top AI researchers, reversing a years-long brain drain to Western tech companies and universities. Improved domestic research infrastructure, competitive compensation, and national AI investment programs are cited as key factors. The trend has implications for the global AI competition landscape.
