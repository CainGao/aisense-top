CNN Sues Perplexity AI Over "Verbatim" Copycat Articles

CNN has filed a copyright infringement lawsuit against AI search startup Perplexity, alleging that the company's AI generates "verbatim" reproductions of CNN's copyrighted news articles. The lawsuit is the latest in a wave of legal actions by major publishers against AI companies, following similar suits from The New York Times, Dow Jones, and others. The case could set important precedents for how AI systems handle copyrighted news content.

Anthropic's Claude Gets Honesty Upgrade for Error Transparency

Anthropic has released a new version of Claude designed to be more transparent when it makes mistakes. The updated model is better at acknowledging when it's wrong, admitting uncertainty, and correcting its own errors rather than doubling down on incorrect answers. The move addresses one of the most common criticisms of AI assistants — their tendency to confidently present wrong information.

Figma Make AI App Builder Now Available for Everyone

Figma has officially launched Make, its AI-powered app building tool, for general availability. Make allows designers and developers to rapidly prototype and build functional applications using natural language descriptions and visual design tools. The tool represents Figma's biggest expansion beyond design into the full development workflow, competing with tools like v0 and Lovable.

Liquid AI Releases LFM2.5-8B: Better On-Device Mixture-of-Experts Model

Liquid AI has unveiled LFM2.5-8B-A1B, an improved on-device mixture-of-experts language model that delivers stronger performance while maintaining efficiency. The model uses a sparse architecture to activate only a fraction of its parameters per inference, making it suitable for deployment on consumer hardware. This release continues the trend of making capable AI models run locally on phones and laptops.

Source: Liquid AI Blog →

Pope Leo XIV Addresses AI's Limits in First Major Technology Statement

Pope Leo XIV has made his first significant address on artificial intelligence, warning about the technology's limitations and ethical implications. The Pope's remarks touched on AI's inability to replace human dignity, judgment, and moral reasoning. The Vatican's engagement with AI ethics continues under the new papacy, building on the groundwork laid by Pope Francis who previously called for international AI regulation.
