1. Anthropic Raises $65B Series H at $900B Valuation

Anthropic closed a massive $65 billion Series H funding round, pushing its valuation to nearly $1 trillion at $900 billion — surpassing OpenAI's last known valuation of $730 billion. The company says funds will advance safety research, expand compute infrastructure, and scale products. This marks a pivotal moment in the AI race, as Anthropic cements its position as the most valuable AI company globally.

Why it matters: The valuation gap between top AI labs and the rest of the tech industry continues to widen, with safety-focused AI now commanding premium valuations.

2. Microsoft Launches Copilot Health AI Preview

Microsoft rolled out a preview of Copilot Health AI, a tool that can analyze medical records and provide clinical insights. The product integrates with existing healthcare workflows and aims to assist medical professionals with diagnostic support and patient data interpretation.

Why it matters: Healthcare AI is entering a critical phase where major tech platforms are moving from research to production-grade tools, raising both opportunity and regulatory scrutiny.

Read More → The Verge

3. Illinois Set to Enact Broadest US State AI Safety Law

Illinois Governor JB Pritzker announced plans to sign an AI safety bill passed by the state legislature, mandating independent audits and whistleblower protections at AI companies. The law goes beyond recently enacted measures in New York and California, establishing the most comprehensive state-level AI regulation in the US.

Why it matters: State-level AI regulation is accelerating, creating a patchwork of compliance requirements for AI companies operating across the US.

Read More → The Verge

4. OpenAI Codex Expands Computer Use to Windows

After launching on macOS, OpenAI's Codex computer use feature is now available on Windows. The tool can "see" your screen and perform tasks on your device autonomously. Users can also manage and review Codex jobs remotely through the ChatGPT mobile app.

Why it matters: Computer use agents are becoming cross-platform, moving from experimental to everyday productivity tools across all major desktop OS.

Read More → OpenAI Developers

5. Microsoft Building AI "Super App"

Microsoft is reportedly developing an AI super app that combines GitHub Copilot, the Copilot chatbot, Copilot Cowork, and a new agentic workflow capability internally named "Autopilot" into one unified interface. The move mirrors OpenAI's own super app ambitions and signals a consolidation trend in AI tooling.

Why it matters: The "super app" race in AI is heating up — whoever owns the primary AI interface will control the next computing paradigm.

Read More → Fortune

6. Figma Make Edits Production Codebases

Figma Make now allows teams to connect to production or sandbox repositories and use Make as a visual surface for building and editing real software. A new editing panel enables precise design adjustments for layouts, colors, font sizes, and effects. This bridges the gap between design and development in a way no other tool has achieved.

Why it matters: Design-to-code tools are reaching production maturity, potentially reshaping frontend development workflows.

Read More → The Verge

7. Amazon Powers AI Shopping for Other Retailers

Amazon is now selling access to the technology behind its Alexa for Shopping assistant, enabling other retailers like Kate Spade to build their own AI shopping chatbots. The AI interface offers personalized recommendations with images, pricing, and store policy answers.

Why it matters: AI-as-a-service for e-commerce is becoming a revenue stream for big tech, democratizing conversational shopping experiences.

8. Google Shares Gemini Chats via Drive

Google is rolling out a feature that lets users share Gemini chat snapshots through Google Drive's sharing interface. Recipients can continue the conversation without altering the original thread. The feature starts rolling out June 3rd.

Read More → Google Workspace Blog

9. OpenAI Sunsets ChatGPT Canvas Interface

OpenAI announced that the Canvas feature — which allowed side-by-side editing of code or text with ChatGPT — will no longer be available with GPT-5.5 Instant or GPT-5.5 Thinking. Legacy model access remains available for a limited time. OpenAI is also trimming GPT-5.5 Instant response lengths to reduce "bullet-heavy" output.

Read More → The Verge

10. DuckDuckGo Installs Surge After Google AI Search Overhaul

Following Google's AI-heavy Search redesign at I/O, DuckDuckGo reported a 33% week-over-week increase in US iOS installs. Visits to its "No AI" search platform also jumped up to 27.7%. The backlash signals meaningful user demand for AI-free search alternatives.
