Anthropic Raises $65B at $900B Valuation, Surpassing OpenAI

Anthropic has closed a massive $65 billion Series H funding round, reaching a staggering $900 billion valuation — now higher than OpenAI's last valuation of $730 billion. The round signals unprecedented investor confidence in AI safety-focused development. Anthropic says the funds will go toward advancing safety research, expanding compute infrastructure, and scaling its enterprise product lineup. The valuation leap makes Anthropic one of the most valuable private companies in history.

Microsoft Launches Copilot Health AI for Medical Records Analysis

Microsoft has launched a preview of Copilot Health AI, a new tool capable of analyzing patient medical records to surface insights for healthcare providers. The system integrates with existing hospital data systems and uses AI to summarize patient histories, flag potential concerns, and suggest follow-up actions. Microsoft says the tool is designed to augment — not replace — clinical decision-making, with built-in privacy safeguards and HIPAA compliance.

Policy

Illinois Set to Enact Broadest US State AI Safety Law

Illinois Governor JB Pritzker has announced plans to sign a sweeping AI safety bill passed by the state legislature. The law would require independent audits of AI systems, mandate whistleblower protections at AI companies, and establish transparency requirements that go beyond recently passed laws in New York and California. The legislation reflects growing state-level momentum to regulate AI development in the absence of comprehensive federal rules.

Figma Make Now Connects Directly to Production Codebases

Figma has expanded its Make AI tool to connect directly with production and sandbox repositories, enabling teams to use a visual interface for building and editing real software. A new editing panel allows precise design adjustments to layouts, colors, font sizes, and effects — all reflected in actual code. The update positions Figma Make as a serious competitor to code-generation tools like v0 and Lovable, bridging the gap between design and production code.

Anti-AI Slop Movement Goes Viral as taste-skill Hits 27K Stars

A grassroots movement against generic AI-generated content is exploding on GitHub. The taste-skill project (27,793 stars, 2,066 today) gives AI coding assistants "good taste" to prevent them from producing boring, cookie-cutter output. Meanwhile, stop-slop by hardikpandya (6,836 stars, 618 today) specifically removes AI "tells" from written prose. Together with the AISlop CLI tool, these projects signal a growing backlash against the flood of homogeneous AI content — and a market opportunity for quality-filtering tools.
